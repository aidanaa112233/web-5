THE STORY BEHIND THE FLAVOR

A Taste of Louisiana

Popeyes is a famous fast-food restaurant brand founded in New Orleans, Louisiana, in 1972.

The brand is known for its crispy fried chicken, delicious sandwiches, signature spices and unique Louisiana-inspired flavors.

Our team chose Popeyes as the inspiration for our Frontend Development project. We wanted to create a modern, responsive and interactive restaurant website.
